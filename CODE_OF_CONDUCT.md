# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Participants should treat other people with respect, accept constructive feedback, take responsibility for mistakes, and focus discussions on maintaining an accurate technical directory.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized language or attention, trolling, insults, personal or political attacks, publishing private information, and conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct. They may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for harmful behavior.

## Scope and reporting

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the project. Report problems through the contact options on the [jqueryscript GitHub profile](https://github.com/jqueryscript). Reports will be reviewed privately as far as practical.
